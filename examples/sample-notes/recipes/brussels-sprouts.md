# Brussels Sprouts (Air Fryer)

Quick and crispy air fryer brussels sprouts

## Ingredients

- 1 lb brussels sprouts, halved
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- Salt and pepper to taste
- Optional: balsamic glaze, parmesan

## Instructions

1. Preheat air fryer to 400°F
2. Toss brussels sprouts with olive oil, salt, and pepper
3. Place in air fryer basket in single layer
4. Cook for 15-18 minutes, shaking basket halfway through
5. Brussels should be crispy and golden brown
6. Optional: drizzle with balsamic glaze and parmesan

## Notes

- Make sure to shake the basket halfway through for even cooking
- Don't overcrowd the basket - cook in batches if needed
- Can add garlic powder or red pepper flakes for extra flavor
