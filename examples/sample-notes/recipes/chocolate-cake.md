# Chocolate Cake

Rich and moist chocolate cake

## Ingredients

- 2 cups all-purpose flour
- 2 cups sugar
- 3/4 cup cocoa powder
- 2 tsp baking soda
- 1 tsp baking powder
- 1 tsp salt
- 2 eggs
- 1 cup buttermilk
- 1 cup strong black coffee
- 1/2 cup vegetable oil
- 1 tsp vanilla extract

## Instructions

1. Preheat oven to 350°F
2. Grease and flour two 9-inch round pans
3. Mix dry ingredients in large bowl
4. Add eggs, buttermilk, coffee, oil, and vanilla
5. Beat on medium speed for 2 minutes
6. Pour into prepared pans
7. Bake for 30-35 minutes until toothpick comes out clean
8. Cool in pans for 10 minutes, then remove to wire rack

## Notes

- Use dark cocoa powder for best results
- Coffee enhances the chocolate flavor
- Can be made as cupcakes (18-20 minutes baking time)
